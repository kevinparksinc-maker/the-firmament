## FALSIFIABLE STAT-LEVEL PREDICTIONS

These are testable claims about actual match data. For each, specify:
- The stat you're predicting
- The expected direction/range
- Why the nakshatra profile predicts this

### Possession & Territory
- **Expected possession split:** [e.g., "Side A 60-70%, Side B 30-40%"]
  - Because: [e.g., "Pushya L1 = methodical, controls space; Punarvasu L7 = resilient defense, cedes territory"]

- **Expected shot count:** [e.g., "Side A 16-22 shots, Side B 4-8 shots"]
  - Because: [e.g., "Side A dominates territory; Side B compact, counterattacking"]

### Shot Quality
- **Expected xG (expected goals):** [e.g., "Side A 1.2-1.8 xG, Side B 0.8-1.2 xG"]
  - Because: [e.g., "Low Initiative (Pushya) generates volume but not quality—expect >20 shots but xG <1.5. Low-percentage crosses forced by compact defense."]

- **Expected xG per shot:** [e.g., "Side A 0.06-0.08, Side B 0.10-0.15"]
  - Because: [e.g., "Defensive/Tactical style (Mercury Punarvasu for defending team) forces wide service, low-quality chances"]

- **Expected conversion rate:** [e.g., "Side A 4-7%, Side B 15-25%"]
  - Because: [e.g., "Medium finishing (Punarvasu) + low xG per shot → low conversion despite shot volume"]

### Defensive Shape
- **Expected crosses by Side A:** [e.g., ">25 crosses (above their seasonal average of 12)"]
  - Because: [e.g., "Compact defending (Pushya, Tactical) forces Service A away from central incisive play into wide service"]

- **Expected tackles/clearances by Side B:** [High/Medium/Low relative to season]
  - Because: [e.g., "Excellent Pressure Response (Pushya H4) = holds shape under sustained pressure"]

### Momentum/Timing
- **Expected goal timing:** [e.g., "If Side A scores, expect before 70 min. If Side B scores, expect 70+ min (counterattack/set piece window)"]
  - Because: [e.g., "Low Initiative = slow build, less ruthless early; Resilient (L7) = improves as match wears on"]

- **Expected card count:** [e.g., "Low (4-6 total) / High (10+) / Volatile (unpredictable)"]
  - Because: [e.g., "Volatile temperament (Ardra Sun, Moon) = risk of emotional swings, more cards"]

## WHY EACH PREDICTION, NOT JUST NARRATIVE

**Bad (vague):**
> "Germany lacks aggressive drive and can't break through Paraguay's defense."

**Good (specific, falsifiable):**
> "Germany generates volume (18-22 shots) but low quality (xG 1.2-1.6, ~0.08 per shot) due to Low Initiative (Pushya L1) and Tactical/Defensive style forcing wide service against compact defense (Pushya H4, Tactical Mercury). Paraguay's Excellent Pressure Response holds shape and converts clinical counter-chance. Expect >25 crosses, <5% conversion, Paraguay goal on break or set piece."

The second version:
- ✅ Can be checked against actual stats
- ✅ Specifies direction of error (shots, not xG; volume, not quality)
- ✅ Can't be reinterpreted afterward ("oh, I meant bad shots not no shots")
- ✅ Identifies specific mechanism (wide service forced by compactness)

## Examples

### Germany vs Paraguay (Tightened Version)

**PREDICTED WINNER:** Paraguay

**CONFIDENCE:** 75% (not 93.75% — game is close)

**Possession:** Germany 60-70%, Paraguay 30-40%

**Shots:** Germany 18-24, Paraguay 5-8

**xG:** Germany 1.2-1.6 (low quality), Paraguay 0.7-1.0 (clinical)

**xG per shot:** Germany 0.06-0.08, Paraguay 0.10-0.15

**Conversion:** Germany 4-7%, Paraguay 15-25%

**Crosses:** Germany >25 (forced by Paraguay's compact shape)

**Why:** 
- Pushya L1 (Germany) = methodical, generates volume, lacks incisive quality
- Pushya H4 + Mercury Punarvasu H7 (Paraguay) = rock-solid defensive foundation, excellent under pressure, clinical on rare chances
- Result: Germany dominates territory but can't finish; Paraguay defends perfectly and punishes once

**Why 75% not 93.75%:**
- Games this close statistically go either way (even with 1-0 edge, penalties exist)
- Confidence should reflect tightness, not chart margin

## Key Rules

1. **One prediction per stat, not one prediction per game**
   - Bad: "Paraguay will win"
   - Good: "Germany >20 shots, xG <1.5; Paraguay 0 shots, 1 goal on counterbreak"

2. **Specify ranges, not point estimates**
   - "19-23 shots" not "21 shots"
   - "0.06-0.09 xG/shot" not "0.075 xG/shot"
   - Leaves room for normal variance, falsifies if you're truly wrong

3. **Include "why" for each prediction**
   - Links the nakshatra profile to the specific stat
   - Makes it falsifiable: if xG/shot is 0.12 (higher quality than predicted), the mechanism was wrong

4. **Don't use vague words**
   - ❌ "Can't break through"
   - ✅ "xG <1.5 and >25 crosses (forced wide service)"
   - ❌ "Resilient defense"
   - ✅ ">8 clearances, <0.8 xG allowed, pressure response holds until 70+ min"

5. **Separate outcome prediction from mechanism prediction**
   - Outcome: "Paraguay wins" (ternary: A wins / B wins / draw)
   - Mechanism: "via defensive solidity + clinical finishing vs. wasteful dominance"
   - Both can be right, both can be wrong, only one can be right but mechanism wrong (lucky)

---

## Why This Matters

With vague predictions, you can always find a post-hoc narrative that fits:
- Germany had no shots? "See, no aggressive drive"
- Germany had 21 shots? "See, wasteful and inefficient"
- Paraguay won? "Resilience!"
- Germany won? "Well, possession doesn't always equal goals..."

With stat-level predictions, you can't:
- Either the xG was 1.2-1.6 or it wasn't
- Either there were >25 crosses or there weren't
- Either the conversion was 4-7% or it wasn't

This is the difference between **prediction** and **storytelling**.
