### 2. Mechanism: Partial Hit

**Predicted:**

- Match shape: **Partially wrong.** The system predicted a cleaner win (straights, "not close") but the actual match had a middle-set reset where Sinner reasserted before Mensik took control again.

**Why the mechanism partially failed:**
The system correctly identified Mensik's structural advantage (Mars in Dhanishta H9 = "Aggressive, Fast, High finishing," Saturn/Moon in Uttara Bhadrapada = "High consistency, High PR"). But it didn't predict the volatility that showed up — Sinner had a dominant 6-2 middle set, suggesting his skill/peak performance was still real, just outweighed by Mensik's consistency and serve.

The system predicted dominant throughout. Reality was: dominant-weak-dominant, but dominant enough to win.

**Calibration check:**

## Where the Mechanism Fell Short

The system said "not close, straight sets, dominant serving." Reality was "3 sets, competitive until the end, but dominant serving."

**Why?** The system correctly read Mensik's serve dominance (Mars in Dhanishta = Aggressive, Fast, High finishing) but underestimated Sinner's capacity for a peak set even if overall structurally weaker (Venus in Shatabhisha = Opportunistic, still capable of execution in isolated moments).

The system predicted the wrong *shape* of victory, but got the right *outcome*. Mensik's Mars/Dhanishta aggression did dominate the opening and closing, but Sinner's Venus/Shatabhisha opportunism found a window in the middle.
